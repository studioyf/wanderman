WanderMan layouts, draft 1

Three ways to lay out the phone, and one for the Mac, all showing Monday 23 in Berlin as you planned it. The pace warnings and walking order are round 2 features, drawn here so the layout is judged with them in place. The look is Paper for all of them; the look is a separate choice. Pick a direction, or a mix (for example A with B's day strip), and say what feels wrong.

A The map, with the day in a sheet Recommended

The map fills the screen; the day sits in a sheet you pull up or push down with your thumb, the way Apple Maps works, so it is already learned. On foot the map is what you navigate by, and the list is always one thumb away. Four tabs along the bottom, where the thumb is: Today, Places, Trip, Notes. A tapped stop opens as its own card in the same sheet, with Done and Later in reach. The trade-off: the list never has the whole screen unless you pull the sheet all the way up.

B The day as a list, the map as a strip

The day reads top to bottom like your Notion page, with a strip of days to swipe across and a short map above that opens full screen on a tap. It is the calmest to read and the closest to how you plan. The trade-off: on the street, finding your way means one more tap to open the map.

C Cards on the map

The map fills the screen and the stops are cards you swipe through along the bottom, the map following each card, like browsing places in Google Maps or Airbnb. The most visual, and good for "what is next". The trade-off: only one stop is visible at a time, so the shape of the whole day is harder to see.

+ Moving things around, a quick tidy, and a list to pick from

Moving: hold a stop and drag it up or down; swipe it right for Done, left for Later (later today, another day, or next time). Tidy: one button that puts the day back in walking order from where you are, and, when the day is fuller than your pace, offers what to push, showing the day before and after so you accept it in one tap or leave it. To pick from: everything saved for the trip but not on a day, grouped by area, the ones near today's walk first; tap + to put one on today, or hold it to choose a day. Drawn in A, the same in B and C. On the Mac it is the middle column's second tab, and stops drag from it onto a day in the sidebar.

M On the Mac: three columns Recommended

A sidebar with the trip's days, its areas, the Inbox and the arrival guide; the chosen day or area as a list in the middle; the map on the right, as large as the window allows. Planning is moving things between days and seeing them on the map, so both stay on screen at once; a stop clicked in the list or on the map opens as a card floating over the map. On the iPad the same layout, the sidebar folding away in portrait.
